About Us

Everybody deserves a foundation strong enough to build a life on. It takes a place to stand.

Too many people leave treatment or incarceration with nowhere stable to go — and without stability, everything else falls apart. Rising Light Foundation was built on a simple belief: give someone a safe home and a real skill, and you give them a foundation strong enough to build a life on.

"Every person who walks through our door is met with respect, not judgment — because recovery isn't a program you complete, it's a life you rebuild."

We're led by people with lived experience in recovery and hands-on skilled trades — so the support we offer isn't theoretical. It's earned.

Our Model

How a distressed house becomes a fresh start

Our approach connects safe housing directly to job skills — participants don't just live somewhere while they wait for life to change. They help build the change.

01

We acquire a distressed property

Rising Light Foundation purchases homes in need of repair in and around Monroe County, often through foreclosure or acquisition programs available to nonprofits.

02

The home is rehabbed and made livable

The property is brought up to a safe, livable standard so a participant can move in — training begins on the next property, not the one someone is living in.

03

A participant moves in, stable and supported

Housing comes with structure: peer support check-ins, accountability, and connection to recovery and mental health resources.

04

They train by rehabbing the next home

Once settled, participants learn real skilled-trades work by helping restore the next property — building a resume and a trade, not just waiting out a program.

Programs

Three pillars, one foundation

Every part of our work supports the same goal: a stable, sustainable path forward.

🏠

Recovery Housing

Safe, stable housing for adults in recovery from addiction or reentering life after incarceration — a real home, not a waiting room.

🔧

Skilled Trades Training

Hands-on training in construction and remodeling, taught by people with real experience in the field — building a trade participants can carry for life.

🤝

Peer Recovery Support

One-on-one support from people who've lived it — regular check-ins, accountability, and connection to community resources.
